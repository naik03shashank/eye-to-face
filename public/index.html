Eye → Face

Add a close-up eye photo and a face photo. The eye becomes ASCII, the camera dives into the pupil, and the face appears among the stars. The animation runs on your device. If you pick a story genre, your face photo is sent to our server and on to Google’s Gemini to generate the artwork; we do not store it.

1 · Eye photo

The red ring marks the pupil the camera will dive into. If it is off, click the centre of the pupil.

2 · Face photo

Tightercrop

The dashed box is what gets turned into ASCII. Click the face to centre it and use the slider so the face fills the box.
